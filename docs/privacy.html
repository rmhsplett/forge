Forge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Forge is a workout-tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. Your privacy is simple: Forge does not collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Forge has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We (the developer) never receive your information.

Where your data lives

All of your workouts, programs, body-weight entries, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync in a future version, that data is stored in your own private iCloud account, which only you can access.

Apple Health

With your permission, Forge reads your heart rate (during workouts on Apple Watch) and body weight, and can save completed workouts and weigh-ins back to Apple Health. This exchange happens only on your device, through Apple's HealthKit. Forge never transmits health data anywhere. You can grant or revoke this access at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings, and Forge works without it.

Photos

If you choose a background photo, it stays on your device and is used only to display behind the app.

Children

Forge is rated 4+ and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
