## Description
Forge is a focused training log for lifters who want signal, not clutter.

Built for serious strength and conditioning work:

• Log sets fast — weight, reps, and per-side left/right tracking for dumbbell
  and single-limb work.
• Strength and conditioning in one app — classic set-and-rep training plus
  Circuit and AMRAP formats with round tracking and interval timers.
• See real progress — estimated 1RM charts with personal-best markers, weekly
  training volume per muscle, and a smoothed body-weight trend.
• Build your own programs — create and edit routines day by day, or start from
  the built-in split.
• Smart rest timers — separate compound and isolation durations that start the
  moment you complete a set.
• Band-assisted and bodyweight-plus-load logging, plus a kettlebell movement
  library.
• Make it yours — accent colors, font styles, and your own background photo
  with adjustable frosted panels.

On your wrist:
• Run the whole workout from your Apple Watch — check off sets, track rounds,
  and get haptic rest cues.
• Live heart rate during every session, with results flowing back to your phone.

Private by design:
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no trackers.
• Your data stays on your device. Body weight and heart rate are read from and
  written to Apple Health only with your permission — nothing is sent to us.

## App Privacy (App Store Connect → App Privacy)
Answer: **Data Not Collected.**

Rationale: all workout data, body weight, and heart rate are stored locally on
the device. HealthKit data is read/written to the user's own Health store with
permission. Nothing is transmitted to the developer or any third party, and
there are no analytics or advertising SDKs.

A **privacy policy URL is still required** for health apps. Minimum viable text
for that page:

> Forge does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. All workout,
> body-weight, and heart-rate information is stored on your device. Forge reads
> and writes Apple Health data only with your explicit permission. Forge
> contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

## App Review notes (paste into the Review Notes field)
> Forge uses HealthKit to (1) read heart rate during a workout on Apple Watch,
> (2) read/write the user's body weight for the progress chart, and (3) save
> completed workouts to Apple Health. All processing is on-device; no health
> data leaves the device or is sent to the developer. HealthKit access is
> optional — the app is fully usable without granting it. There is no login;
> demo data is seeded on first launch so all features are visible immediately.
